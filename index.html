ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Your next chapter shouldn't start after graduation.

The CBE program under UCHS was built to dismantle the gap between school curriculum and real-world success. Rather than waiting for a diploma to discover what fuels you, CBE empowers students to test ideas, engage in meaningful professional environments, and forge lasting networks right now.

HAVE QUESTIONS?

Curious? Start here.

Quick, clear answers to common inquiries about the CBE program.

What is the CBE program? + −
CBE (UCHS Program) is an educational initiative that bridges high school learning with real-world professional development, hands-on experience, career exploration, and valuable industry networking.
Who can participate? + −
The program is open to eligible students enrolled in the school community who are motivated to explore career opportunities, gain practical skills, and take ownership of their personal and professional growth.
How do I get started? + −
Start by connecting with your school counselor or program advisor, reviewing the track pathways, and requesting the official enrollment and intake details.
Where can I ask more questions? + −
You can reach out directly to the program coordinators at the campus counseling office or use the official contact channels detailed in the footer below.
